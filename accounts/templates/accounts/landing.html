About the City

Baliwag City, Bulacan

Founded in 1733 and officially recognized as a city in December 2022, Baliwag has grown into one of the most progressive centers of commerce, transportation, and education in Northern Bulacan. Known as the "Buntal Hat Capital of the Philippines," the city blends rich Spanish-era heritage with modern civic development.

Today, Baliwag is home to over 175,000 residents across 27 barangays. CityWatch was built to support the city's continued growth — giving every resident a direct channel to help keep their community safe, clean, and well-maintained.
